summary: "We propose LPA-MoE, a lightweight period-aware Mixture-of-Experts model for time-series forecasting in data-center refrigeration scenarios. It integrates FFT-based periodic embeddings with sparse MoE routing to decouple periodic variations, and uses a gated lightweight TimesBlock with depthwise separable convolutions for fine-grained temporal feature extraction. Experiments on real-world IoT datasets from data-center refrigeration units show improved prediction accuracy and stability on multi-pattern time-series data."
